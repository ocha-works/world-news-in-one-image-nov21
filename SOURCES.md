# Sources — the reporting behind the front page

Every story on the image happened, or was first reported, on
Saturday 21 November 2026, and each was cross-checked in at least
two outlets (searched and read on 21 November 2026). Headlines below
are the outlets' own. A note on dates: several of the day's stories
are Friday 20 November events in US/European time that were first

## Panels

2. **World Court: Myanmar committed genocide** — the
   International Court of Justice found Myanmar guilty of genocide
   against the Rohingya, the first time any international court has
   found a state guilty of genocide. The ruling was delivered on
   Thursday 19 November (9–6) and was still the lead justice story
   on 21 November, with Myanmar's military-backed government
   dismissing it on Friday and the UN chief welcoming it.
   - UN News, 20 Nov 2026: "World's top court finds Myanmar guilty
     of genocide against Rohingya" (three-hour ruling; Myanmar's
     security forces intentionally committed acts under Article II
     of the Genocide Convention)
   - CNN, 19 Nov 2026: "In historic first, UN top court rules a
     country committed genocide" (first time the ICJ has held a
     state accountable for genocide in its 80-year history; 9–6;
     Myanmar's spokesperson dismissed the ruling on Friday)
   - Human Rights Watch, 19 Nov 2026: "World Court Finds Myanmar
     Committed Rohingya Genocide" (first time the ICJ found a
     country responsible for committing genocide)
   - FIDH, 19 Nov 2026: "Myanmar: World court issues historic
     first judgment recognising Rohingya genocide"
   - Note: the ruling itself was delivered 19 November, not
     21 November; it is included because it was the week's defining
     justice story and still led the 21 November day pages.
